# MoodFeed Privacy Policy (GDPR / KVKK)

**Last updated:** August 28, 2026

## 1. Data Collection & Transparency
MoodFeed collects minimal and necessary information to optimize your mental well-being and social media feed:
- Account profile data (Email, display name, avatar, bio)
- Content data (Posts, comments, likes, saves)
- Emotional classification metadata (Mood AI scores between -1.0 and 1.0)
- Essential analytics and diagnostic metrics

## 2. Zero Unnecessary Retention
We do not sell personal data to advertisers. Ranking preferences and emotional filters belong exclusively to the user.

## 3. GDPR & KVKK Rights
- Right of Access & Data Export (Downloadable in CSV format via `/v1/analytics/export`)
- Right of Rectification
- Right of Erasure ("Right to be forgotten")
- Right to Object to automated processing

For privacy inquiries: `privacy@moodfeed.app`
